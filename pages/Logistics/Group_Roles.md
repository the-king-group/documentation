# Group Roles

*note: descriptions are subject to change*

## Resources
- Who orders things
- keep track of what we need i.e. inventory
- Also tracks the computing allocations

## Communications
- Runs the calendar and teams
- Making sure people have access to the teams and calendar
- Keeps track of group meeting and journal club schedule - who is presenting/what are we talking about
- In charge of room reservation each quarter for group meeting

## Documentation
- Keeping the documentation and github up to date and add new things as they come up 
- Assist in onboarding
- Data storage 
- Website updates 

## Onboarding
- Gets new members up and running
- Helps them get set up with a desk and keys
- Helps them talk to the resource person with what they need to buy if anything to set up their desk
- Gets them started on the tutorials
