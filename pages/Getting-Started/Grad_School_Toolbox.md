# Grad School Toolbox

Things to help you get set up and organized as you start grad school.

## Where do things live?

As a grad student you'll be taking notes, writing out ideas, saving data files, writing code, making files, and otherwise producing a high amount of electronic and written work. Think about where will these things live. Will you have a physical lab notebook, an electronic one? Will you do class work on a tablet, a notebook, or on your laptop? What type of file system will you use so in 4 years you can still find the things you saved today? Thinking about these questions now and setting up your system for saving your notes/ideas/etc. will set you up for organizational success.
